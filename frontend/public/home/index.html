Is MediConnect free for patients?

Yes. Searching, booking, requests and reminders cost nothing. You pay the doctor's fee at the hospital.

What is a partner hospital?

A hospital whose doctors keep their hours on MediConnect, so you can book a free slot at once.

Can I book at other hospitals?

Yes, by request. The hospital accepts or declines it, and your list shows which.

Where does hospital information come from?

From OpenStreetMap, Wikidata and each hospital's own website. Every page shows the source and when it was read.

Can I use MediConnect in an emergency?

No. In an emergency call 112, or 108 for an ambulance.
